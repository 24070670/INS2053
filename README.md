# INS2053 - Web Authoring and Web Management

Workspace ca nhan de luu ghi chu, exercise, homework, bai on tap va do an theo course repository:
[INS2053 course materials](https://github.com/hieutachi/INS2053-Web-Authoring-and-Web-Management-IS_VNU_Course/).

## Cau truc

```text
INS2053/
|-- course-plan/                 # Lich hoc, muc tieu va ghi chu tong quan
|-- weekly-work/
|   |-- session-01/ ... session-15/
|       |-- notes/               # Ghi chu bai hoc
|       |-- exercises/           # Thuc hanh tren lop
|       |-- homework/            # Ma nguon va tai lieu homework
|       `-- submission-evidence/ # Video phan anh, anh chup, link nop bai
|-- assessment-prep/
|   |-- midterm/                 # On tap va bai thuc hanh giua ky
|   `-- final/                   # On tap va bai thuc hanh cuoi ky
|-- capstone/
|   |-- planning/                # Y tuong, sitemap, wireframe, phan cong
|   |-- milestones/M1 ... M8/    # Tien do va dau ra tung milestone
|   `-- website/                 # Ma nguon website Student Club
|       |-- css/
|       |-- images/
|       `-- media/
`-- final-submission/            # Ban nop cuoi cung va file ZIP
```

## Cach su dung

- Doi chieu so session voi `schedule.md`, `exercises/session-NN/` va `homework/session-NN/` trong course repository.
- Luu rieng bai thuc hanh va homework theo tung session; dat bang chung nop bai vao `submission-evidence/` cung session.
- Ghi tien do do an trong `capstone/milestones/M1` den `M8`. Cac trang HTML cua Student Club dat truc tiep trong `capstone/website/`, ben canh `css/`, `images/` va `media/`.
- Dat file nop cuoi cung trong `final-submission/`; doi `[YourName]` va `[StudentID]` theo thong tin cua ban khi dat ten ZIP: `INS2053_[YourName]_[StudentID].zip`.

Thu muc nay chi tao khung lam bai, khong sao chep noi dung bai tap tu repository course.
